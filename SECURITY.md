# Security policy

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability in a DataVedam product or repository, please report it privately. **Do not open a public issue.**

Email **contact@datavedam.com** with the subject line `Security report`, and include:

- the affected product, URL or repository
- steps to reproduce, and the impact you observed
- any proof-of-concept code, screenshots or logs

We'll acknowledge your report, keep you updated while we investigate, and credit you once a fix is released if you'd like us to.
