**DataVedam is an AI products and engineering company.** We build AI products for the workflows businesses already run, and design and engineer new systems for the ones they haven't built yet.

<br>

### Products

| Product | What it does |
|:--|:--|
| **[PracharVedam](https://datavedam.com/pracharvedam)** | Marketing and growth workspace: brand context, creative production, market intelligence, lead workflows and measurement. |
| **[Axaria](https://datavedam.com/products/axaria)** | Voice agents for business conversations, including support intake, scheduling and inbound sales, with verification, tool boundaries and human handoff. |
| **[ShikshaVedam](https://datavedam.com/shikshavedam)** | School operations workspace with role-based views for attendance, academics, fees and communication. |

### Technology

| Platform | What it does |
|:--|:--|
| **[Sarva](https://datavedam.com/technology/sarva)** | A governed runtime for product agents. Tool access, approvals, persistence and traceable execution are built in. |
| **[BodhiVedam](https://datavedam.com/technology/bodhi)** | Knowledge distillation: teacher-to-student training, evaluation and deployment packaging for smaller, faster models. |
